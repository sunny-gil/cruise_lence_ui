Grievance Redressal Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

1. Introduction

Cruise Lens Academy (“CLA”, “we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to maintaining a transparent and student-first approach in all operations. This Grievance Redressal Policy (“Policy”) ensures that all grievances raised by students, applicants, or stakeholders are addressed promptly, fairly, and transparently. This Policy is in line with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, Information Technology Act, 2000, IT Rules 2021, and RBI guidelines for payment-related grievances.

2. Scope of the Policy

This Policy applies to all grievances related to:

  • Admissions, fees, and payments through integrated gateways.
  • Training quality, curriculum delivery, and faculty conduct.
  • Certification, assessments, and academic results.
  • Documentation support and maritime compliance.
  • Placement support and job assistance processes.
  • Website, portal, or digital access issues.
  • General complaints regarding services or conduct.

3. Objectives of the Policy

  • Provide a clear mechanism for raising concerns.
  • Ensure timely acknowledgment and resolution of grievances.
  • Promote fairness, accountability, and transparency.
  • Maintain records for compliance and continuous improvement.

4. How to Raise a Grievance

Students or stakeholders may raise grievances via:

  • Email: contact@cruiselensacademy.com
  • Phone: +91 73875 24800 (10:00 AM – 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday)
  • In-Person: Submit written grievance at our head office in Mapusa, Goa.
  • Grievance Form: Submit via official online form (if available).

Every grievance must include:

  • Full Name and Enrollment ID (if applicable)
  • Contact details (phone and email)
  • Detailed description of the issue
  • Supporting documents (receipts, screenshots, communications)
  • Preferred mode of resolution

5. Grievance Redressal Process

  • Acknowledgment: Within 48 working hours.
  • Review & Investigation: Relevant department reviews and may request additional documents.
  • Resolution Timeline: CLA aims to resolve within 30 calendar days; complex cases may take longer with updates provided.
  • Escalation: If not resolved, escalated internally to senior management.

7. Confidentiality

All grievances will be handled confidentially. Only those responsible for investigation and resolution will access the details. CLA assures that raising a grievance will not result in retaliation or adverse impact.

8. Record Keeping

  • Nature of grievances raised
  • Acknowledgment and communication logs
  • Investigation steps
  • Final resolution and timelines

9. Force Majeure Situations

Delays due to events beyond CLA’s control—natural disasters, pandemics, government restrictions, or technical failures—may extend resolution timelines. Students will be informed of such delays.

10. Policy Updates

CLA reserves the right to revise this Policy anytime. The updated version will be available on the CLA website with the effective date. Continued use of services after updates constitutes acceptance.
