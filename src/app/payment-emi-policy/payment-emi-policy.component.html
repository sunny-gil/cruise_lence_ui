Payment & EMI Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

1. Introduction

The Payment & EMI Policy of Cruise Lens Academy (“CLA”) sets out rules governing fee payments, installment facilities, and student financing options. By enrolling and making any payment, you (“student”, “you”) agree to comply with this policy.

2. Course Fees and Currency

All course fees are quoted in Indian Rupees (INR) and payable according to the official fee structure. Fees are exclusive of applicable government taxes (GST) unless stated.

3. Advance / Booking Amount

To secure admission, students must pay a non-refundable advance/booking amount at registration. Payment confirms a seat, subject to eligibility verification and timely document submission.

4. Balance Fees and Due Dates

Balance fees must be paid before batch commencement unless EMI is approved. Late or non-payment may result in cancellation of admission, denied training access, or withheld certification.

5. Payment Methods

  • Integrated RBI-compliant payment gateways (credit/debit card, UPI, wallets, net banking)
  • Direct bank transfer to CLA’s official account
  • Third-party EMI financing through NBFC partners
  • Cash payments beyond legally permitted limits are not accepted

6. EMI and Financing Options

  • Available through authorized NBFCs (e.g., Eduvanz, Propelld, GrayQuest)
  • Approval, rejection, and loan terms are determined by the financing partner
  • EMI agreements are strictly between student and partner; CLA only verifies enrollment and fee
  • EMI defaults must be settled with the financing partner; CLA bears no liability

7. Refunds and Cancellations in EMI Cases

  • Advance/booking amount is non-refundable
  • Cancellations after EMI approval require settlement of dues as per loan agreement
  • Batch cancellations by CLA refund balance fees only, coordinated through the financing partner

8. Default in Payment

  • Suspension from classes until dues are cleared
  • Withholding of results, assessments, or certification
  • Termination of admission without refund
  • Reporting defaults to financing partner may affect credit scores

9. Dispute Resolution

Payment gateway disputes: Contact CLA at contact@cruiselensacademy.com. EMI/loan disputes: Contact the financing partner directly. CLA will provide enrollment verification but will not mediate contractual disputes.

10. Confirmation of Payment

Students receive a digital receipt within 24–48 hours of successful payment. EMI-linked confirmations are also provided by the financing partner. Retaining receipts is the student’s responsibility.

11. Policy on Chargebacks

Unauthorized chargebacks or reversals without exhausting CLA’s grievance process are prohibited. Fraudulent chargeback attempts may result in enrollment termination and legal action.

12. Governing Law and Compliance

Governed by Consumer Protection Act, 2019, Information Technology Act, 2000, and RBI guidelines. All transactions comply with Indian laws.
