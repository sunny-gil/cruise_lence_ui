OUR FOUNDATION

OUR STORY – WHERE PHOTOGRAPHY MEETS THE SEA

Cruise Lens Academy was established in 2025 with a vision to create opportunities for talented photographers to build careers beyond the traditional land-based photography industry.

We recognized that many aspiring and working photographers have creativity, technical ability and passion but often lack access to structured opportunities that can take their careers to a global stage.

Cruise Lens Academy was created to bridge that gap.

Our programs combine professional photography training with cruise industry exposure, guest interaction, professional standards and career-focused preparation. Through this approach, we prepare photographers not only to improve their craft but also to understand the professional environment in which cruise photographers work.

We don't just teach photography. We prepare photographers for opportunities at sea and careers connected to the global cruise and hospitality industry.

Rohan Singh

Founder & CEO

Rohan Singh worked as a Photo Manager on some of the world's leading cruise lines, traveling to 43 countries and leading multicultural teams onboard. During his time at sea, he recognized the exceptional talent of Indian photographers, but also saw how the absence of structured training and maritime guidance prevented them from accessing global opportunities.

He founded Cruise Lens Academy to create a transparent, university-backed ecosystem that prepares photographers for cruise life, technical standards, guest hospitality, and direct placement.

Suraj Thakre

Partner & COO

Suraj Thakre is an operational strategist with extensive leadership experience in client management, team mentorship, and scaling institutional processes. At Cruise Lens Academy, he oversees admissions, candidate counseling, and end-to-end operational execution.

Suraj works closely with every student to ensure seamless support through coursework, STCW maritime preparation, and cruise line interviews, building an empowering environment where students achieve their fullest potential.
