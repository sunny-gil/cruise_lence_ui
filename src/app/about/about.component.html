ABOUT CRUISE LENS ACADEMY

Bridging the gap between Indian photography talent and the global cruise ship industry.

WHO WE ARE

Cruise Lens Academy was founded with a simple but powerful vision — to bridge the gap between Indian photographers with ambition and the global cruise ship industry’s demand for talent.

Our founder, Rohan Singh, began his journey as a cruise photographer and went on to travel across 43 countries while working onboard some of the world’s most renowned cruise lines. Rohan experienced firsthand how rewarding — yet how difficult to access — the world of cruise photography can be for aspiring Indian photographers.

Recognizing this gap, Rohan built Cruise Lens Academy to create a dedicated pathway for Indian talent. The Academy equips students not only with creative skills in photography and editing, but also with essential maritime documentation (STCW, INDOS, SID, Medical), grooming, and direct placement support — everything needed to step confidently onboard.

OUR VISION

To be the leading global academy that transforms passionate Indian photographers into certified, world-class cruise photographers, opening up paths to explore the globe while mastering their craft.

OUR MISSION

To empower Indian photographers by making cruise photography careers accessible, structured, and sustainable. We aim to create opportunities that extend far beyond land, giving our students the tools to capture the world while earning and growing professionally.

OUR VALUES

WHAT DRIVES US

OPPORTUNITY
