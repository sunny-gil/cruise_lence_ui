Academic Integrity Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

1. Introduction

At Cruise Lens Academy (“CLA”), academic integrity is the foundation of trust between students, faculty, and future employers. By enrolling, you (“student”, “you”) agree to uphold honesty, originality, and accountability in all academic activities.

2. Purpose of the Policy

  • Encourage honesty, fairness, and accountability in learning
  • Ensure assessments reflect genuine performance
  • Prevent misconduct like plagiarism, cheating, or impersonation
  • Preserve trust of cruise line employers in CLA-certified graduates

3. Expectations of Integrity

  • Examinations & MCQ Tests: Personal knowledge only; no cheating or unauthorized material
  • Practical Assignments & Studio Sessions: Independent work or instructed group work; no misrepresentation
  • Editing & Software Tasks: Completed individually unless permitted
  • Role-Play & Simulations: Honest participation without falsification
  • Documentation & Records: All submissions must be authentic; forgery is prohibited

4. Forms of Academic Misconduct

  • Plagiarism: Using others’ work without acknowledgment
  • Cheating: Unauthorized notes, devices, or collaboration
  • Impersonation: Taking assessments on behalf of others
  • Fabrication: Falsifying results, attendance, or documents
  • Unauthorized Collaboration: Group work when individual effort is required
  • Tampering with Evaluation: Altering scores or manipulating assessment systems

5. Consequences of Misconduct

  • Written warnings or probation
  • Reduction in marks or failure in assessment
  • Redo assignments or retake tests
  • Suspension from modules/classes
  • Denial of Certificate of Completion
  • Ineligibility for job assistance or placement
  • Expulsion in extreme cases

6. Responsibility of Students

  • Submit only original work; cite references
  • Refrain from unauthorized tools or shortcuts
  • Clarify doubts regarding acceptable collaboration
  • Report observed cheating or plagiarism to faculty

7. Faculty & Academy Responsibility

  • Design fair and transparent assessments
  • Educate students on citation and originality standards
  • Monitor and report misconduct
  • Investigate suspected violations confidentially

8. Link to Certification & Placement

Breaches of integrity affect employability. Serious misconduct can result in denial of certification and job placement assistance, ensuring only honest and competent students are recommended to employers.

9. Appeals and Grievance

Students may appeal accusations under the Grievance Redressal Policy in writing with supporting evidence. Cases are reviewed fairly with communication of decisions in a reasonable timeframe.
