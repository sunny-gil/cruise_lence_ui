Program Overview

The program combines photography fundamentals with cruise photography workflow, guest interaction, professional editing, image presentation and sales fundamentals.

Designed specifically for candidates seeking high-earning international maritime careers, every module is optimized for the actual daily requirements of world-leading luxury cruise liners.
