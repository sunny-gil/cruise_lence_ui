Pathway Overview

Embarking on a cruise ship photography career requires far more than just knowing how to hold a camera. It’s about understanding maritime life, adapting to a multicultural workplace, mastering documentation, and building technical creative skills that can withstand the fast-paced demands of shipboard life.

Our Beginner & New to the Sea course is a comprehensive, career-launch program designed for individuals who are passionate about photography but have never worked at sea. This program provides everything a student needs to become cruise-ready: from mandatory maritime documentation to professional photography and editing training, as well as soft skills, mock interviews, and placement support with international cruise lines.

We keep our batch size small (6–8 students only) to ensure personalized mentoring. By the end of this course, you will have not only the skills but also the confidence to step onboard and thrive in one of the most rewarding careers the travel industry has to offer.

Maritime Services

  • INDOS Registration
  • STCW Certificates: PST, FPFF, EFA, PSSR, STSD, CM
  • SID (Seafarer Identity Document)

Creative & Technical Photography Training

  • DSLR mastery (exposure triangle, white balance, lighting setups)
  • Cruise-specific scenarios: deck portraits, gangway photography, studio sessions, event coverage
  • Roleplay exercises simulating real onboard conditions

Editing & Software Training

Daily 1-hour dedicated practical session

  • Adobe Lightroom (color correction, workflow efficiency)
  • Adobe Photoshop (retouching, compositing)
  • Adobe Bridge (workflow management)
  • IRIS shooting and editing (onboard guest photo systems)

Career Preparation & Placement Assistance

  • Grooming & communication workshops
  • Mock HR interview + employer-specific training
  • Direct interviews with our cruise partners
