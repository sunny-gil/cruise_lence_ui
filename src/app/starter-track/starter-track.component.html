STARTER TRACK: PHOTOGRAPHY & CAMERA BASICS

We focus on structured learning, practical exposure, and disciplined training — helping students understand photography from the ground up before moving into advanced or career-focused programs. The Starter Track is designed as a foundation program for beginners who want clarity, confidence, and correct photographic habits.

Photography Fundamentals

  • ⚓ Camera basics & handling
  • ⚓ Exposure control (ISO, shutter, aperture)
  • ⚓ Composition fundamentals
  • ⚓ Lens & sensor understanding

Lighting & Studio Basics

  • ⚓ Natural & artificial light concepts
  • ⚓ Speedlight fundamentals
  • ⚓ Studio lighting & modifiers
  • ⚓ Hands-on studio sessions

Editing Basics

  • ⚓ Lightroom fundamentals
  • ⚓ Photoshop overview
  • ⚓ Color correction
  • ⚓ White balance

Outcome

  • ⚓ Confidence with camera & light
  • ⚓ Strong photography basics
  • ⚓ Ready for advanced learning

Is this the right course for you?

You should join if you are:

  • ⚓ An absolute beginner in photography
  • ⚓ Someone who has never used a DSLR or mirrorless camera
  • ⚓ Confused by camera settings and technical terms
  • ⚓ Interested in learning photography properly from basics
  • ⚓ Planning to pursue photography more seriously in the future

No prior experience required

No professional camera required to start

Days 1 – 4

Academy-Based Classroom Training

Camera basics, exposure & composition, lenses & settings, lighting & speedlight basics, and editing fundamentals (Lightroom & Photoshop).

Days 5 – 6

Studio-Based Practical Training

Studio lighting setup, modifiers, portrait practice, troubleshooting, and final assessment.
