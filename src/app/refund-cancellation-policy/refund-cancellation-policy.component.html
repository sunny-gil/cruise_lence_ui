Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

1. Introduction

This Refund & Cancellation Policy (“Policy”) sets out the framework governing all fees paid to Cruise Lens Academy (“CLA”) for admission, training, documentation, and associated services. By making a payment to CLA, you (“student”) agree to abide by this Policy.

2. Advance / Booking Amount – Strictly Non-Refundable

The advance or booking amount payable at registration is strictly non-refundable in all circumstances, including withdrawal, personal emergencies, documentation delays, visa or job rejections, or change of mind.

3. Refund of Balance Fees

  • If CLA cancels a batch before commencement → balance fees refunded (excluding advance).
  • If student withdraws after commencement → no refund.
  • If terminated for misconduct → no refund.

4. Mode of Refund

Refunds are processed only via the original payment mode within 15–30 working days. CLA is not responsible for bank/gateway delays.

5. Cancellation Requests & Procedure

Requests must be submitted in writing to contact@cruiselensacademy.com with:

  • Full name & enrollment ID
  • Date/mode of payment
  • Proof of payment (transaction ID/screenshot)
  • Reason for request

Verbal or WhatsApp requests will not be considered.

6. Disputes & Chargebacks

Students must follow CLA’s dispute process before initiating chargebacks. Unauthorized chargebacks may lead to delays or administrative charges.

7. Interview & Job-Related Rejections

Rejections after interview/medical/visa are external factors. No refund is applicable. CLA provides training & assistance, not employment guarantees.

8. Force Majeure & Exceptional Circumstances

No refunds in cases of natural disasters, pandemics, or government restrictions. CLA may reschedule or switch to blended/digital classes.

9. Student Financing / EMI Payments

Loan agreements are directly between students and NBFC lenders. EMI defaults or cancellations do not entitle refunds from CLA.

10. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

Policy governed by laws of India. Disputes fall under exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Goa, India.
