Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

1. Introduction

This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how Cruise Lens Academy (“CLA”) manages the distribution of receipts, learning materials, certificates, and other documents. By enrolling, you agree to this Policy.

2. Nature of Deliverables

Digital Deliverables:

  • Admission confirmations and payment receipts
  • Course schedules, joining instructions, and orientation documents
  • Learning resources, assignments, and evaluation reports
  • Certificates of Completion in digital format
  • Placement-related communication and interview calls

Physical Deliverables (only when required):

  • Printed certificates or transcripts (on request)
  • Orientation kits or hard-copy brochures (if introduced)
  • Branded materials (if part of official packages)

3. Mode of Delivery

  • Digital: Sent to registered email or uploaded to student portal; delivery deemed complete upon dispatch/upload confirmation
  • Physical: Dispatched via reputed courier/postal service to provided address

4. Delivery Timelines

  • Digital Deliveries:
    • Receipts and confirmations → 24–48 hours of payment
    • Learning materials & schedules → within 7 working days of batch confirmation
    • Certificates → within 10 working days of completion & dues clearance
  • Physical Deliveries:
    • Dispatched within 7–10 working days of course completion or request approval
    • Timelines may vary due to location, courier workload, and external factors

5. Responsibility of Students

  • Provide accurate email for digital communication
  • Provide correct postal address (PIN & contact number) for physical delivery
  • Ensure availability at address for acceptance
  • Inform CLA of any changes in contact details
  • Failure to update details may cause delays, for which CLA is not liable

6. Failed or Returned Deliveries

  • Digital: Notify CLA if emails bounce; reattempt after issue resolution
  • Physical: Incorrect/incomplete addresses may require re-shipping charges for a second dispatch

7. Liability & Force Majeure

CLA is not responsible for delays/failures caused by third-party courier services, internet failures, or force majeure events (strikes, lockdowns, natural disasters). Delays outside CLA’s control do not justify refunds or damages.

8. International Deliveries

Currently, CLA does not offer international physical deliveries. Students abroad receive all documents digitally. Future international deliveries will incur additional courier charges and extended timelines.

9. Confirmation of Delivery

  • Digital: Delivery complete once email/portal dispatch is successful; server logs may serve as proof
  • Physical: Delivery complete once courier confirms handover or records a delivery attempt; tracking shared where available
