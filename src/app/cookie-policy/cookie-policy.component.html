1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Cruise Lens Academy (“CLA”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and digital platforms. By continuing to use our website, you (“user”, “visitor”, “student”) consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this Policy.

Cookies help us improve your browsing experience, remember your preferences, analyze website traffic, and deliver a more personalized experience. Some cookies are essential for our site to function correctly, while others are used for analytics and improvement.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. These files allow websites to recognize your device, store preferences, and provide a more seamless experience.

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies such as web beacons, tracking pixels, and local storage objects to achieve similar purposes.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential to enable navigation and use of secure features like forms, login, or payment gateways.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Collect anonymous information (pages visited, time spent, errors) to improve functionality and user experience.
  • Functionality Cookies: Remember user preferences (language, location, login) for a customized experience.
  • Advertising & Marketing Cookies: Track browsing behavior to deliver relevant ads or measure campaign effectiveness. CLA uses remarketing tools only when campaigns are run.

4. Why We Use Cookies

  • Ensure the website operates securely and efficiently.
  • Improve navigation, speed, and responsiveness.
  • Understand visitor behavior and identify areas for improvement.
  • Personalize content and remember user settings or login sessions.
  • Facilitate secure payment transactions via integrated gateways.
  • Measure effectiveness of campaigns and engagement on digital platforms.

5. Managing & Disabling Cookies

Most browsers automatically accept cookies. However, you can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings:

  • Browser Settings: Usually found under "Privacy" or "Security" options.
  • Blocking Cookies: Some site features may not function properly if cookies are blocked (e.g., login, forms, payments).
  • Deleting Cookies: Clearing cookies removes stored preferences and requires re-entry of details on next visit.

6. Third-Party Cookies

CLA may use trusted third-party services that place cookies on your device, including:

  • Payment Gateways: Secure transaction processing and fraud prevention.
  • Analytics Providers: Analyze site usage patterns (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Social Media Plugins: Allow sharing or integration of content on platforms like Facebook, Instagram, or LinkedIn.

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies. CLA is not responsible for third-party cookies, and usage of external services is subject to their policies.

7. Data Security & Privacy

Cookies do not give us access to your personal files or confidential data. Any personal information collected via cookies is processed in line with our Privacy Policy, ensuring compliance with the Indian IT Act, 2000 and related rules.
