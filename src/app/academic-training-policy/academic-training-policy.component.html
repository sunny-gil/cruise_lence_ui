Academic & Training Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

1. Introduction

The Academic & Training Policy of Cruise Lens Academy (“CLA”, “we”, “our”, “us”) sets out the standards and responsibilities expected of every student. By enrolling with CLA, you (“student”, “you”) agree to comply with these terms regarding academic expectations, attendance, assessments, certification, and conduct.

2. Training Philosophy

CLA integrates theory with practical exposure and industry relevance. Students undergo classroom lectures, studio practice, editing workshops, maritime compliance sessions, and role-plays. This ensures readiness for cruise ship employment with confidence and professionalism.

3. Attendance and Commitment

Students must maintain at least 90% attendance. Genuine emergencies must be communicated with valid documentation. Excessive absenteeism or habitual tardiness may lead to disqualification from certification.

4. Curriculum and Delivery

The curriculum covers fundamentals of photography, advanced cruise-specific techniques, editing software (Lightroom, Photoshop, IRIS), role-plays, and maritime documentation. CLA may update modules or schedules to improve learning without entitlement to refunds.

5. Faculty and Learning Environment

Faculty are experienced cruise photography professionals. Students must maintain respect, participate actively, and uphold cooperative learning. Misconduct may result in disciplinary action.

6. Assessments and Evaluation

Students are evaluated through assignments, practical tasks, exams, editing tasks, role-plays, and HR interview rounds. Minimum overall score of 80% is required for job assistance eligibility. Certificates are still issued but placement support is conditional.

7. Certification

Certificates are issued only when attendance, assessments, discipline, and dues requirements are met. CLA reserves the right to withhold certification if requirements are not fulfilled.

8. Student Conduct in Academic Settings

  • Arrive on time and actively participate
  • Respect facilities, equipment, peers, and faculty
  • Mobile phones only for academic use
  • No unauthorized recording or sharing of CLA content
  • Misbehavior may lead to warnings, suspension, or termination without refund

9. Academic Integrity

Cheating, plagiarism, impersonation, or tampering with evaluation systems is prohibited and may result in denial of certification, expulsion, or loss of job assistance eligibility.

10. Documentation and Compliance

Students are responsible for submitting accurate maritime documentation (INDOS, STCW, SID, medicals). CLA facilitates the process but is not liable for delays, rejections, or incomplete submissions.

11. Intellectual Property and Learning Materials

All training materials remain CLA intellectual property. Unauthorized copying, sharing, or commercial use may result in disciplinary action and legal consequences.

12. Consequences of Policy Violations

Violations may result in warnings, suspension, denial of certification, or termination of enrollment. CLA decisions are final.

13. Grievance Redressal

Academic grievances such as assessment disputes or faculty behavior concerns may be raised via CLA’s Grievance Redressal mechanism. Students will receive a fair investigation.
