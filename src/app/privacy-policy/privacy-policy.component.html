Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Cruise Lens Academy (“CLA”, “we”, “our”, “us”) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect the personal information you provide when you access our website, enroll in our programs, make payments, or participate in any of our services.

This policy aligns with:

  • Information Technology Act, 2000, and IT Rules, 2011.
  • Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • Global GDPR-style data protection principles.

2. Information We Collect

We collect various categories of personal data to deliver services effectively and comply with regulations.

a) Identity & Contact Information

  • Name, gender, date of birth.
  • Address, phone number, email ID.
  • Government-issued IDs (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport).

b) Educational & Professional Data

  • Academic transcripts, certificates, resumes.
  • Employment or seafarer experience (if applicable).

c) Payment & Financial Data

  • Transaction details such as payment reference, amount, and status.
  • Bank/card details processed securely by payment gateways; not stored by CLA.

d) Medical & Maritime Data

  • Medical certificates, STCW records, INDOS/SID details.

e) Technical & Usage Data

  • IP address, browser type, and device information.
  • Cookies and analytics data for improving website performance.

3. Data Sharing & Cookies

We do not sell or rent your data. Information is shared only with trusted partners under confidentiality agreements:

  • Payment Gateways/Banks: Transaction processing.
  • Training Partners: Handling maritime documentation.
  • Cruise Lines/Employers: Placement assistance.
  • Government Authorities: As legally required.
  • Service Providers: Hosting, analytics, communications.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to:

  • Remember preferences and login sessions.
  • Analyze usage and improve navigation.
  • Enhance personalization and user experience.

4. Data Storage, Security & Rights

All data is stored securely in India using encryption, firewalls, and controlled access. While we strive for top-level security, no system is entirely risk-free.

Data Retention

  • Training & admission data retained until certification completion.
  • Maritime documents as required by law or partners.
  • Financial records for minimum 8 years per Indian tax laws.

Your Rights

  • Access or correct your personal data.
  • Request deletion where lawful.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications.
  • Request data portability when applicable.

5. Additional Provisions

Third-party hosting or analytics providers may process data internationally, always under contractual safeguards.

Children’s Data

Our services target adults (18+). If a child’s data is submitted, parents may request deletion by contacting us.
