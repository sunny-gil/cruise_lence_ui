Currently Working Cruise Photographers

Consultation & Evaluation Program

For photographers who are already working onboard cruise ships, Cruise Lens Academy offers a unique evaluation & consultation program. This course is designed to test your existing skills, review your portfolio, and benchmark you against global cruise photography standards.

The purpose of this program is to ensure working photographers continue to grow in their careers, get access to better cruise companies, and remain competitive in an evolving market.

Instead of starting from scratch, we give professionals an honest evaluation of their technical ability, creativity, and documentation status, and then guide them toward the right next step.

This consultation ensures that if you’re already onboard, you’re not left behind — you’ll either move faster into higher-level cruise photography roles, or you’ll get a roadmap for upgrading your skills with us.

1. Portfolio & Skills Evaluation (Paid):

Online Test + Technical Photography

Review of technical photography, editing, guest engagement.

Scoring out of 100.

2. Results Pathway:

≥80% Score:

Eligible for HR interview with our cruise company partners

<80% Score:

Recommendation to join Course 2 (STCW Holder / Ex-Seafarer) for upskilling.

Free re-evaluation after completing Course 2.

3. Confidential Career Guidance:

Personal feedback on strengths & weaknesses.

Documentation guidance (if any renewal required).

Tailored roadmap for career advancement.
