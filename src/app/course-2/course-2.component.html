SEAFARER READINESS

MARITIME & STCW TRAINING MODULES

STCW MODULE 1
Personal Safety & Social Responsibilities (PSSR)

Understanding shipboard emergency procedures, safety culture, pollution prevention, human relationships, and working within an international crew hierarchy.

STCW MODULE 2
Elementary First Aid (EFA)

Immediate actions in medical emergencies, CPR techniques, managing shock, dressing wounds, and basic maritime casualty care.

STCW MODULE 3
Fire Prevention & Fire Fighting (FPFF)

Practical fire prevention measures, fire containment, extinguishing techniques, and using breathing apparatus in enclosed spaces.

STCW MODULE 4
Personal Survival Techniques (PST)

Essential survival training in the event of abandon-ship: life jacket deployment, life raft boarding, immersion suits, and open-water survival strategies.

STCW MODULE 5
Security Training for Seafarers with Designated Security Duties (STSDSD)

ISPS Code standards, identifying maritime security risks, surveillance, anti-piracy precautions, and assisting security personnel onboard.
