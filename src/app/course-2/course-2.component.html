STCW Holder / Ex-Seafarer Pathway

Many professionals already possess their STCW certification or have past seafaring experience but now wish to transition into cruise ship photography.

The STCW Holder / Ex-Seafarer course is a fast-track 10-day intensive program that skips documentation and focuses purely on creative, technical, and placement preparation.

Over 10 days in Goa, students undergo roleplays, hands-on sessions, editing workshops, and guest lectures — finishing with an exam & certification.
