Code of Conduct Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

1. Introduction

The Code of Conduct Policy of Cruise Lens Academy (“CLA”, “we”, “our”) outlines behavioral expectations for students. By enrolling, you (“student”, “you”) agree to uphold professionalism, mutual respect, and accountability at all times.

2. Purpose of the Code

  • Establish standards aligned with cruise line expectations
  • Ensure a safe, inclusive, and supportive environment
  • Promote integrity, discipline, and responsibility
  • Protect CLA and student reputation with external partners

3. Professional Behavior

Students must maintain punctual attendance, preparation, respectful interaction, and appropriate use of CLA resources. Dress and grooming should reflect industry standards.

4. Respect and Inclusivity

CLA promotes respect for diversity in background, culture, gender, religion, and opinion. Discrimination, harassment, bullying, or intimidation in any form is strictly prohibited.

5. Integrity and Honesty

Students must act honestly in all academic and non-academic matters. Cheating, plagiarism, impersonation, or misrepresentation during documentation or placements may result in denial of certification, disqualification from job assistance, or termination.

6. Use of Facilities and Resources

Students must use classrooms, studios, labs, and materials responsibly. Negligence may lead to charges. Unauthorized use of CLA content or brand for personal or commercial purposes is prohibited.

7. Digital Conduct

Responsible use of CLA portals, emails, and communication groups is mandatory. Misuse, offensive content, misinformation, or unauthorized recording may lead to disciplinary action. Personal social media content should not harm CLA’s reputation.

8. Prohibited Conduct

  • Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or abusive behavior
  • Use/possession of drugs, alcohol, or prohibited substances on premises
  • Theft, vandalism, or deliberate property damage
  • Unauthorized visitors on premises
  • Disruption of classes or official sessions
  • Any activity endangering self or others

9. Disciplinary Measures

  • Verbal or written warnings
  • Suspension from classes or facility use
  • Withholding assessments or results
  • Termination of enrollment without refund
  • Reporting serious misconduct to authorities/employers

10. Student Responsibility

Students must familiarize themselves with this Code, conduct themselves professionally, and report any serious breaches they witness.

11. Grievance & Appeals

Appeals against disciplinary actions can be made through CLA’s Grievance Redressal Policy in writing within the specified timeline.
